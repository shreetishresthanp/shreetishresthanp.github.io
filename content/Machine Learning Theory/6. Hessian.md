- Derivative of 1D function: slope at a point
- First generalization: gradient: a vector of all partial derivatives
- Hessian: matrix of all second-order partial derivatives
	- How the gradient itself is changing in every direction

The Hessian $H[f] \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ has entries:
$$H[f]_{i,j} = \frac{\partial^2 f(x)}{\partial x_i \partial x_j}$$
So each entry $(i,j)$ asks: how does the $i-th$ partial derivative change as we move in the $j-th$ direction?


- L-smoothness: A function is L-smooth if the gradient doesn't change too fast. L controls the maximum rate of change of the gradient

- Covariance Matrix: captures how the different dimensions of x vary together
	- $(i,j)$ entry tells how dimension $i$ and dimension $j$ co-vary
