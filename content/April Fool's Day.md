It's weird. I thought I'd get used to it by now. The grind, the get-go, the ambition. Truthfully speaking, it's not the ambition that's heavy; it's the chatter around it.

Moving to a city surely makes you feel behind x 10 on any given day. It's not that I see people physically running to get to places, or that I keep hearing things about someone getting their next opportunity, or that everybody keeps talking about the future specifically. 

It's just that everything feels like it's moving so fast and all cars have left the start line while all this time, I've been struggling to get my seatbelt on. It's the conversations about what your plan is, where are your interning for the summer, when are you graduating... It's the talks about research ideas and brainstorming. I don't feel particularly pleasant about the word brainstorming. For once, I'd like my brain to stop storming, for ideas, for thoughts or for rationales. I'd want it to stop rushing to think, to take action, or to make sense. 

I get why Sonu Didi felt overwhelmed and was worried for me. It is, after all, a very fast-paced culture here in the city. Some days I love it, because that's what I wanted, but some days, I wish there was a pause button. It's weird because none of this is imposed on me and yet, it not correct to say it's purely self-imposed either.

This feels like a day I want to go hide in my room. Now, I am mature enough or rather old enough to know that this stems from me not performing as well and keeping tasks pending by procrastinating and if I could just get these tasks started, I'd gain momentum and be fine. I guess that's what people call self-sabotage. That word sounds quite heavy, it's not that I'm hindering my own progress. I do the things that absolutely need to be done, albeit at the last moment. I know the things I need to do to feel better, but I keep postponing them by way of which, I get things done and get by, but never above and beyond.
