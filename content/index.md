> *I’m interested in NLP systems and model interpretability, particularly in understanding the internal mechanisms that give rise to model behavior. More broadly, I’m drawn to questions about how complex models represent information and how their computations produce the outputs we observe.
> 
> I tend to approach technical ideas through intuition first. Modern machine learning is deeply mathematical, and that rigor matters, but in my experience, meaningful understanding begins with clear conceptual models. Once the intuition is in place, the math becomes a tool for refining and extending ideas. Much of the work and writing on this site grows from that perspective.
> 
> Occasionally, I also write about observations and random musings from daily life.*
